# Switchable packed incidence: one physical layout for programmable unit constraints and genuine 3-CNF

Date 2026-11-26. Starting master f33e22439300bc155dd430f171a458d830437564. Extends [programmable unit-tag residuals](programmable_tag_residual_handoff.md), [packed unit separators](packed_separator_handoff.md), and [binary selector elimination](binary_selector_elimination_handoff.md). The objective is to leave the previously analyzed easy lookup/sorting promises while retaining their exact physical-coordinate selector properties. New exact grammar, NP-complete fixed-metadata incidence subfamily, and conditional disjoint gate-reserve transfer. Paper proofs and finite exact replay, not independent audit, Lean formalization, or a new unrestricted superlinear bound. L-clb-036 remains open.

## 1. Switchable clause gadget

Use the standard literal convention (v,1) is positive, (v,0) negative. For every group j of three programmable literals L_(3j), L_(3j+1), L_(3j+2), reserve three distinct guard variables z_i and one common global mode variable p for the entire word. Emit:
    (z_(3j) OR z_(3j+1) OR z_(3j+2));
    (NOT p OR z_i), (NOT z_i OR L_i) for the three i in that group.
Pin p by one unit clause with programmable sign. Guards occur only in these clauses. For fixed values of all variables in the L_i, existential elimination of the z_i gives exactly:
- p=1: all three L_i true (all z_i forced true).
- p=0: at least one L_i true (choose a true literal and its guard true, other guards false).
Therefore for any assignment of all non-guard witness variables, extension to a satisfying guard assignment exists iff the appropriate conjunction or disjunction holds in every group. This is stronger than equality of the SAT verdict; the equivalence also holds under arbitrary pinning of any non-guard variables. No requirement that a satisfying witness be unique. The guard mode is a global switch, not a free separate mode per clause.

## 2. Physical paired grammar, single-copy payloads and size

Let m=2^k be the number of triple groups, 1<=ell<=k, P=2^ell, t=3m programmable literal slots, D=t ell designated physical payload bits. Let Q be the least power of two >=(t+2)P+t+1, w=log2 Q+1. Use the following disjoint namespaces:
- tag 0: Q+a, a in [0,P), dictionary variable pinned to arbitrary h(a) by one unit clause per a;
- tag 1: Q+P+a, a in [0,P), signed witness variables. For each a=2j emit (u_a OR u_(a+1)), (NOT u_a OR NOT u_(a+1)), with u_a=Q+P+a. Thus u_(2j+1)=NOT u_(2j); the even variable is otherwise unforced;
- tags 2,...,t+1: fresh unforced variables for programmable unit constraints or inactive slots;
- z_i=Q+(t+2)P+i for 0<=i<t; p=Q+(t+2)P+t.
The largest ID is <=2Q-1, the smallest >=Q, so every ID has exactly w canonical digits. The low ell digits of Q+tag_i P+x_i are x_i, regardless of tag. Guards and mode IDs do not overlap with any programmable ID.

Emit in fixed order: all P dictionary units; all P binary complement clauses; one mode unit; for each triple emit its guard disjunction, and for each of its three positions emit first (NOT p OR z_i), then (NOT z_i OR L_i). The only occurrence of each payload x_i is as the ell low identifier digits of L_i in this last binary clause. Its literal sign and upper tag digits can be changed independently. Other occurrences of a logical variable with the same identifier do not constitute copies of its physical primary bits. All payload coordinates are independently variable.

Paired grammar: a literal of width w uses [1,sign], w least-significant-first digit pairs [1,bit], and [0,1], length L=2w+4; clause terminates [0,0], adding 2. There are 3P+15m+1 literal occurrences and 2P+7m+1 clauses. Exact word length:
    n(k,ell)=(3P+15m+1)(2w+4)+2(2P+7m+1).
For physical zero-based position, if b_i is the start offset of the second literal of (NOT z_i OR L_i), its low digit j lies at b_i+3+2j. All b_i depend only on m,ell, not on h,p,tags,signs or payloads. Mode sign has its own fixed coordinate. Thus changing any one payload bit changes exactly one physical word bit. All words in the subcube and the metadata contexts below are valid canonical formulas.

For ell=Theta(k), w=k+ell+O(1), n=Theta(mk), D=Theta(n). For ell=k, P=m and n=(72+o(1))mk, D=3mk. For ell=floor(k/4), P=o(m) and n=(37.5+o(1))mk, D=(.75+o(1))mk. These constants reflect substantial guard and dictionary overhead, not optimal packing. All lengths even. The prior fixed tautology padding by IDs 0,1 of lengths 10 and 14 can extend to all sufficiently large even lengths when fixing one of these ell(k) choices: select largest k with n(k,ell(k))<=n-48 and use bounded successive scale ratio and the even padding semigroup. No new padding checker here; the actual unpadded grammar is tested. Do not identify this new physical terminal set with the prior unit-only layout's positions.

## 3. Hard mode with ALL metadata fixed

Set p=0, all tags 1, all literal signs positive, dictionary all zeros (unused). The full formula is satisfiable exactly when
    H_(m,ell)(x)= EXISTS v_0,...,v_(P/2-1)
        AND_{j=0}^{m-1} OR_{i=3j}^{3j+2} (v_(floor(x_i/2)) XOR (x_i mod 2)).
Every one of the D=3m ell physical payload bits is free, and every input assignment is a well-formed instance. Here x_i is an ell-bit index. This is ordinary 3-CNF incidence with signed literals encoded by a variable index and a polarity in the low payload digit. The negative literal in the mathematical 3-CNF is implemented as a positive literal of the complementary witness variable in the physical encoding. The witnesses v are not pinned by the dictionary or computed by deterministic guard propagation.

For ell=k, the length-indexed incidence language H is NP-complete in the ordinary uniform sense. Define the language to reject malformed lengths, accepting exactly the satisfying incidence strings of length 3k 2^k for k>=1. The parameter is recoverable from length; verification uses P/2 witnesses and a polynomial-time check. For reduction from any 3-CNF, compact variable names to v used variables, with c clauses of exactly three literals (repeat literals to pad shorter nonempty clauses). Handle empty clauses/constant instances separately by fixed yes/no inputs. Choose k=ceil(log2 max(2v,c,2)), so m=P>=2v,c and m<2 max(2v,c,2) unless equality at boundary. Encode each literal as 2j plus its sign-complement bit, and pad extra clauses with tautologies (v_0,NOT v_0,v_0). Unused variables free. The incidence data length 3mk and paired word length Theta(mk) are O(c log(c+2)) after compacting names (v<=3c for nonempty formulas), of the same order as the conventional fixed-width indexed representation in the worst case. This is not a claim of constant-factor reduction for every variable-length compressed CNF representation. The restriction is a uniform polynomial reduction, and uses only the payload bits of the fixed metadata subcube. For ell=floor(k/4) the same NP-completeness follows by choosing k large enough that 2^ell>=2v and m>=c, at polynomial rather than near-linear blowup. No known quasilinear-size unrestricted nonuniform circuit upper bound for this incidence family is supplied here; absence of a known upper bound is not a lower bound. NP-completeness does not rule out linear circuits (such a conclusion would require a nonuniform complexity separation, e.g. NP not subset P/poly).

Every payload bit in hard mode is essential when m>=2: choose a group containing its slot and another group. Set all literals in the first group to index 0, all in the other group to index 1, and every remaining group to a tautology. The instance is unsatisfiable because it demands v_0 and NOT v_0. Flip exactly the selected payload bit in the first group. If low bit flips, the new literal is NOT v_0; if a higher index bit flips, the new literal belongs to an independent witness that can be set true. The other group forces v_0=0, and now all clauses can be satisfied. Thus each payload bit has an explicit opposite-verdict adjacent input pair in the same fixed-metadata subcube. No new gate lower bound beyond ordinary essential-support count follows by itself.

## 4. The same physical coordinates retain all earlier programmable-unit obligations

Set p=1. Every L_i is now required true, and the tag-1 complement constraints are satisfiable on their own. Use only dictionary tag 0 and tags >=2 for payload slots. This is exactly the former programmable unit-tag consistency family on t=3m slots, up to independent satisfiable auxiliary constraints. In particular:
- activate one slot at dictionary tag 0, h(a)=a_j, sign either phase, and give all others distinct nonzero tags >=2. This retrieves ANY designated payload bit or its complement, and all other payloads are globally irrelevant in this context.
- use equal tags >=2 and opposite signs to realize every disjoint union of complete bipartite conflict components; all unused slots receive fresh tags.
- combine graph constraints with an arbitrary dictionary test of one slot, independently.
The previous proofs of exact graph residual counts a_t and core-reserve bounds therefore apply to this new set of physical positions, not by identifying them with the old layout, but by exact semantic equivalence in unit mode. There are exactly a_t distinct graph residuals in that subfamily even with one heterogeneous retained bit per slot; the recurrence is in programmable_tag_residual_handoff.md. For full data ancestry A(S), S all D payloads, previous operand-slot theorem gives
    |A(S)| >= D-1+ceil(log2 a_t).
For ell=floor(k/4), this is (5-o(1))D, but only about n/10 on this higher-overhead layout. For ell=k it is (2-o(1))D; the positive/negative singleton contexts also imply >=2D for D>=3 by the previous tight normal form. The new point is coexistence of the structural selector/graph obligations and arbitrary 3-CNF on EXACTLY the same free physical coordinates, not a numerical improvement on earlier full SAT size lower bounds.

## 5. Conditional hard-core reserve theorem on original gate IDs

Model ordinary proper binary Boolean gates, signed wires and constants free, with syntactic ancestry in designated data bits. Let S be any nonempty subset of the physical payload bits, s=|S|. Let J be a set of r>=1 triple groups for which ALL 3r ell payload bits are in S. Denote d_J=3r ell. Let C_B(H_(r,ell)) be the ordinary minimum binary gate count for the fixed-metadata incidence function on r clauses (r need not be a power of two). Then
    |A(S)| >= 2(s-d_J) + C_B(H_(r,ell)).
Here A(S) is the set of live original binary gate IDs reachable from any physical bit in S. This is a conditional transfer of a smaller hard incidence lower bound, not a proof that the hard core itself has superlinear complexity.

Proof:
1. Fix all payload bits outside S to zero. Keep allowed metadata contexts consisting of singleton retrieval of every S bit (unit mode), and one hard core context (existential mode, all slots in groups J tag 1, positive; every slot outside J has a distinct tag >=2, positive). The inactive outside groups always have a satisfying extension independently of ANY outside payload values, since all their literal namespaces are fresh and disjoint from tag 1, and positive. Their clauses do not constrain the hard core. The hard context thus computes H_(r,ell) solely on the core bits, independent of every S bit outside it. The allowed contexts are legal on the full free S cube.
2. Delete each S bit outside J in any order. Retain the hard context and all singleton retrieval contexts of surviving bits, discard contexts selecting the removed bit. Every bit is essential before deletion at its own retrieval context, and at least one surviving bit is essential afterward at a retained singleton context. All retained contexts ignore the deleted bit for every remaining data assignment. The previously proved rank-free two-gate elimination rule removes at least two distinct original counted IDs per deleted bit, including the legitimate parity-reader substitution case (force the sole reader constant without adding a circuit). No new original ancestry is introduced.
3. After s-d_J deletions, fix metadata to the retained hard context. Query-only gates now become constants; the surviving actual circuit computes H_(r,ell). Its binary gate count is >=C_B(H_(r,ell)), and every remaining live gate has ancestry in S in the original graph, with IDs disjoint from all eliminated charges. Count the removed and retained IDs.

It is essential to set outside groups to fresh namespaces in the hard context BEFORE the deletions, not simply assume arbitrary outside bits form tautologies. Fixing a single outside value would not justify the parity-reader substitution. This construction works for arbitrary independently varying outside payloads.

In fact the remaining circuit must still realize the selected core's graph/dictionary and singleton contexts if those are kept during the deletion, since unit mode can make all noncore slots inactive as well. Thus replace the C_B term above by the maximum of that term and any certified lower bound from the programmable-tag core family. One may also use actual topology-specific lower bounds on the surviving cone, or maximize over different J. One may NOT add hard-core and graph lower bounds on the SAME surviving gates, or sum different J charges without a separate disjointness proof.

This transfer is compatible with the original SAT circuit at unpadded or previously padded lengths, since the restriction only fixes physical coordinates and performs certified deletion/simplification. It offers a concrete common-coordinate hard target for future structural arguments. It does not magically give a new exponent: if only C_B>=d_J-1 is known, the resulting lower bound is linear. A small circuit for full SAT would give circuits for H, as expected; the extra information is the explicit two-gate charge for each excluded physical data coordinate and the coexistence of arbitrary selector contexts. We still need a structural argument that yields a growing gate cost per retained input, or a cross-context incompatibility of the remaining gate ledger.

## 7. Next research target and limitations

The prior unit-only, forced-lookup, affine and sorting promises had explicit easy upper constructions. The new switchable layout contains an NP-complete fixed-metadata incidence subfamily, while retaining the exact same physical payload positions for rich programmable contexts. This is a meaningful pivot, not a superlinear lower-bound proof. A useful next target is to derive nontrivial lower bounds on H from its local contradictory-clause neighborhoods, or to exploit essential-bit edge witnesses in the hard subcube alongside deletion-stable selectors in unit mode. The specific premise needing proof is an incompatibility of surviving gate roles or a superlinear hard-core bound, not merely high residual entropy, NP-completeness, or a large number of witnesses. The exact core reserve charges only disjoint removed and retained gate IDs. No claims of improvement over the strongest repository-wide SAT lower bounds, novelty priority, independent audit, Lean check, or production implementation. L-clb-036 remains open.
