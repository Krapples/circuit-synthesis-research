# Comparison with the public pequalnp research programs (2026-11-26)

Review of https://pequalnp.com/programs, the live homepage, https://pequalnp.com/how, the published HTML note https://pequalnp.com/papers/note-1, and selected public Lean files. Local baseline commit 0a0a611b547927dfc658a70004a0a0533ae5833c. This is a targeted comparison, not an exhaustive audit of the thousands of upstream proofs or a literature priority determination. The dynamic programs page and desk-row endpoints did not expose their full records to the web reader; public source files were retrieved via GitHub. No Lean builds rerun, no new mathematical theorem, no upstream submission, no production changes.

## Directly comparable results

- Public L-clb-037 establishes full support at length 6q; L-clb-039 proves counted fan-in-two AND/OR/NOT size >=6q-1 for q>=2; L-clb-041 proves >=5k+1 at length 4k+2 for k>=4. Read the actual files:

  https://github.com/open-goat/pequalnp-proofs/blob/main/lean/PEqualNP/CircuitLowerBounds/L039.lean
  https://github.com/open-goat/pequalnp-proofs/blob/main/lean/PEqualNP/CircuitLowerBounds/L041.lean
- Local [encoding bounds review](../NOTES_SAT_ENCODING_BOUNDS.md) and [root research notes](../../RESEARCH_NOTES.md) report end-to-end Lean results SatBridge.sat_six_lower' (>=8q-3 at n=6q) and sat_robust_identifier_eventually (2n-O(n log log n/log n) on multiples of six, via explicit integer loss). The external SAT language and external counted circuit model are used by model transfer. These are strict improvements over L039 and asymptotically over L041 on common lengths (n=6q, q odd), not a claim that no stronger upstream result exists. Public code search found L037/L039/L041 and no exact robust theorem-name match, but search is incomplete. Local notes document passed builds and axiom audits, not independently rerun here. Other even residues remain open in Lean. Proposed 5n/3 gadget additivity remains unproved. None of the linear improvements proves L-clb-036.
- Public note-1 includes a counted-NOT forest edge-function bound |E|+tau(G)-1 (L104; source file inspected), exact path/cycle sizes (L102/L106), upper construction L105/L154 and stronger local graph results. Pair funnel (three of four pair cofactors equal) is directly related to our pair test in guard_dependency_handoff.md. Their extremal least-circuit reader and pruning lemmas exploit minimum size, so our two-primary-read normal form for arbitrary normalized AIGs at constant overhead does not refute their minimum-size rigidity claims. Counts in free-complement AIG, counted NOT and monotone graph representation models must not be equated.

## Our complementary directions

- [sat_weight_slice](../results/sat_weight_slice/README.md): grammar-specific reduction to monotone guarded weight layer, exponent preserving for alpha>1 by the classical slice compiler. Ordinary monotone CLIQUE lower bounds do not automatically transfer; orbit-averaging obstruction matters.
- [andreev_projection](../results/andreev_projection/README.md): independently audited near-cubic formula bound; small AIG for the restriction, so formula unfolding cannot simply charge distinct gates.
- [packed_separator_handoff.md](packed_separator_handoff.md), [packed_welllinked_handoff.md](packed_welllinked_handoff.md), [packed_directional_handoff.md](packed_directional_handoff.md): paper proofs for original paired SAT graph, linear terminal well-linkedness, quadratic planar/fixed-minor-free lower bounds, direction-dependent lookup communication. Standard separator/communication methods specialized to physical encoding; not unrestricted lower bounds.
- [cross_history_charge_handoff.md](cross_history_charge_handoff.md) and earlier causal/feedback/joint notes: phase-specific equivalence and optimized shared-gate flow charge, negative controls. Promising engineering and topology-specific invariants; no independent audit or Lean proof of latest claims, no demonstrated universal superlinear numerical improvement.
- Local exact synthesis has reproducible benchmark evidence and scoped formal results, but public project is not a single competing solver; no head-to-head superiority claim. Some of our native work is already merged upstream (see root research notes for PRs 22166, 22337); do not double-count shared results as independent advances.

## Recommended action

1. Prioritize upstream-compatible formalization and submission of the already locally checked robust SAT lower bound. First audit exact statement against current pinned upstream imports and look for a matching desk gap. Outside-proof submission rules require exactly the posted statement and allowed imports, one file per gap; no lease or PR opened in this review. If no gap, propose a new statement through the appropriate upstream discussion rather than submit an unrelated proof.
2. Bring their reader/constant/dead-reader savings into our stable-ID specialization and regional exact synthesis. Keep counts basis-specific. Prove combined savings for unions of deleted original IDs (no double count). Test against native recursive regional/coupled reader baselines. Their live report mentions L-meta-18545 and L-meta-18649; exact desk statements not fetched, so audit before importing.
3. Test crown/5-cycle rigidity questions using our exact enumeration and phase-first completion, only after importing exact definitions and target budgets. A claim about all minimum circuits cannot be inferred from small finite witnesses or refuted by constant-overhead normal forms.
4. Formalize the causal-cut reconstruction theorem as a reusable standalone lemma, followed by cross-history alias correctness. Start with finite DAG cuts and distinct ports, then max-flow and exact rational certificates; don't begin with the large numerical LP pipeline. Use upstream red-team review. Keep long-run research on simultaneous existential-witness restrictions, where lookup controls no longer suffice.
5. Adopt their verification discipline: paper, computational evidence, machine-checked exact statement, independent/human review separated; explicit counterexamples and dependency tracking. In particular their lemma count is not a probability of solving P vs NP. Our stronger restricted-model or local results do not resolve the hard link or P vs NP.

Public homepage snapshot reported 8,214 checked lemmas and 5,139 refutations, with L-clb-036 still hard link. The site is live and those counts are transient. No ranking of overall progress or novelty claim.
