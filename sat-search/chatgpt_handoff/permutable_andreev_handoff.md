# Research handoff: distributed high-path obligations without exponent loss

Base: `Krapples/circuit-synthesis-research`, commit `870ffb7`, `sat-search/chatgpt_handoff/README.md`, §§1–4. Read also `sat-search/results/sat_weight_slice/README.md`. ## Result

In the signed-edge AND/OR (equivalently AIG) model, let a single-output circuit of size s compute exact-grammar SAT at sufficiently large even length n. At least Ω(n) distinct primaries have output-path count Ω(n²/((log n)² log log n)). At least Ω(n) distinct internal gates have output-path count Ω(n³/(s (log n)² log log n)). Paths count operand occurrences, including parallel edges; the output has its zero-length path. Thus the s=O(n^1.01) case requires Ω(n^1.99/((log n)² log log n)) paths at each of Ω(n) gates, without the earlier n^(-δ) loss. The same result holds on the repository's guarded Hamming slice, replacing n with N.

External premise: Tal, TR14-048 revision 1 (2016-04-19), Lemma 7.1, Theorem 7.2: https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2014/048/revision/1/download/ . Treat the lemma as a theorem premise, not a Lean axiom. Formula size means De Morgan leaves. A primitive-XOR circuit's original paths are not covered by this direct transfer.

## Construction

Use the original packed Andreev construction with M=2^k, ell the largest power of two <= max(1,floor(k/2)), P=2^ell, B=M/ell. Replace the selector row length with r=ell J where J=floor(M/(k ell)). There are kJ full chunks. No partial chunks remain. Allocate all auxiliaries (A=k(2J-1)+2B-1) at Q,...,Q+A-1 where Q is the least power of two >= max(4P,A). All auxiliary identifiers have width w=log2 Q+1 and are disjoint from U_p=P+p and V_p=2P+p. Hence the chunk block (-z OR U_p), (z OR -U_p) always has bit length 20+4w+4(ell+1). Emit all kJ chunk blocks consecutively after the U dictionary, with z identifiers permuted arbitrarily, before fixed XOR chains. Every physical chunk's ell source bits occur in exactly the same two sets of digit positions for every permutation; the auxiliary ID digits are fixed constants in each projection. The data dictionary and guard tree are unchanged. All source occurrence sets are disjoint. Data coordinates appear once and selector coordinates twice. Every permutation assigns exactly J chunks to each row, with fixed XOR chains defining logical row parity.

A=O(M/k), Q=O(M), identifiers have O(k) bits. There are O(M/k) literals including P ell (log2 ell+1)=o(M/k) dictionary literals. Thus n_k=Θ(M). For sufficiently large even n choose largest k with n_k<=n-48. Since n_{k+1}>n-48 and n_{k+1}=O(2^(k+1)), M=Θ(n) without monotonicity assumptions. Append tautologies of length 10 and 14 to achieve n. Balance using `SatBridge.weightSliceWord` if needed: N=3n+12, t=2n+5, copies doubled, signs changed in second occurrence set. The padding and balancing do not depend on the permutation.

## Proof of high-path distribution

Let W(x)=sum of primary path counts over a physical source bit's occurrence set. For threshold T call x light if W(x)<T. If at least half of k r selector source bits are light, count light bits per physical chunk. Sort counts a_0>=...>=a_(kJ-1). Assign rank j*k+i to logical row i, chunk j. Then S_i=sum_j a_(jk+i) is the count of light bits assigned to row i. The max-minus-min difference is sum_j(a_(jk)-a_(jk+k-1)) <= a_0-a_(kJ-1) <= ell, by telescoping the disjoint intervals. Therefore every S_i>=r/2-ell>=r/3 once J>=6.

Let R=floor(r/3), keep exactly R light bits in each row and set the rest to zero. Fix the data table to a hard k-input truth table. Tal + counting implies formula leaf lower bound c M R²/log k for some absolute c>0. Set T=c M R/(2 k log k). The unfolded restricted circuit simplifies to at most sum of W over selected bits <kRT=c M R²/(2 log k), contradiction. Thus more than half the selector bits are heavy. Choose one actual primary with path count >=T/2 (T/4 for balanced layer) per heavy source bit. Distinct source occurrence sets give distinct primaries. Since M=Θ(n), k=Θ(log n), this establishes the primary threshold.

For h=Ω(n) selected primaries with p(i)>=tau, total reader incidence <=2s. At least h/2 have reader degree <=4s/h. For each such primary the recurrence p(i)=sum over direct consumer operand occurrences of p(g) provides a gate with p(g)>=tau h/(4s). A single gate can be selected by at most two distinct primaries, so >=h/4 distinct witnesses. This proves the internal threshold.

## Barrier and next question

The entire reconfigurable family (union over all permutations, data and selectors, at a fixed k/padded length) has an O(n log² n) promise circuit. Extract each slot's first auxiliary identifier (key) and chunk parity (payload). Sort the O(M/k) records by logical identifier with a sorting network (O(m log² m) comparators, O(k) Boolean gates per comparator), XOR the consecutive J parities for each row, and use a multiplexer tree on the fixed data coordinates. The same proof above applies to circuits correct just on this promise, so even the stronger high-path requirement cannot exclude quasi-linear circuits solely on the permutable family. This is an analytic upper bound, not a measured circuit synthesis result. Do not confuse it with an upper bound for all SAT inputs.

Investigate genuinely SAT-specific constraints outside this promise, e.g. simultaneously handling programmable clause incidence in which assignment values are not forced by dictionaries and a deterministic acyclic evaluation. Look for an argument that charges distinct gates rather than counting downstream paths or multiplying cone costs. No such semantic-to-gate-count bridge is proved here.
