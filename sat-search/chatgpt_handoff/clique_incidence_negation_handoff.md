# Single-copy CLIQUE incidence and nonmonotonicity requirements

Date 2026-11-26. Base e5ea1c615d7a59c6025f541f1623470fd2e4d210. Extends switchable_incidence_handoff.md. Explicit monotone projection of the existing hard-mode incidence family, transfer of published monotone bounds, and two necessary structural nonmonotonicity conditions for hypothetical polynomial-size SAT circuits. No unrestricted superlinear gate lower bound, independent audit, Lean proof, production modification or priority claim. L-clb-036 remains open.

## 1. Fixed-coordinate CLIQUE projection

N graph vertices, 2<=K<=N. Introduce existential variables f, s_i, a_ij (one per undirected edge), and t_ij for threshold counting. Pin f=false by the triple (NOT f,NOT f,NOT f). Pad every shorter clause with positive f. For each i<j put two triple clauses in fixed lexicographic order:
    (a_ij XOR x_ij, f, f)
    (NOT s_i, NOT s_j, NOT a_ij).
Here the notation in the first clause means choose the positive literal a_ij when the INPUT bit x_ij=0 and negative when x_ij=1, not an existential XOR gate. Thus a_ij=1-x_ij. In signed witness index notation the first literal is 2*index(a_ij)+x_ij. Its low physical identifier bit is literally x_ij, NOT its complement. Every occurrence of a_ij in other clauses has fixed index/sign. The second clause forbids selecting both endpoints of an absent edge.

For threshold, let t_(i,j) represent at least j selected among first i vertices; t_(i,0)=NOT f, t_(0,j)=f for j>=1. For i=1..N and j=1..K enforce
    y = a OR (b AND x)
with y=t_(i,j), a=t_(i-1,j), b=t_(i-1,j-1), x=s_i.
Four clauses (NOT a OR y), (NOT b OR NOT x OR y), (NOT y OR a OR b), (NOT y OR a OR x) suffice, padding with f. Require t_(N,K)=true. All these clauses have fixed signed witness indices. Exact satisfaction iff the graph contains a K-clique: any satisfying selection has size>=K and all pairs present; any K-clique supplies selected witnesses, edge pins, threshold states and guard extensions.

There are O(N^2+NK)=O(N^2) clauses and variables, also Omega(N^2) clauses. Choose m a power of two at least number of clauses and twice number of variables, pad with (f,NOT f,f), ell=log2 m. Use the previous switchable grammar with p=0, all programmable tags 1, signs positive, and dictionary zero. Its complementary tag-1 variables encode the signed literals above. Consequently exactly binom(N,2) designated low payload coordinates remain free, one per edge, with positive orientation and no copies. All other physical bits fixed. n=Theta(m log m)=Theta(N^2 log N). For multiple K values at a fixed N one may choose a common m large enough for K=N; the pin/edge clause prefix and its variable allocation are identical, so edge coordinates coincide for every K. No claim that all lengths or padding have been newly replayed.

## 2. Published monotone lower bound transfer

External premise: Cavalar, Kumar, Rossman, Monotone Circuit Lower Bounds from Robust Sunflowers, Algorithmica (2022), Theorem 5, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9705498/ . For fixed delta in (0,1/3), K=N^(1/3-delta) (integer rounding), monotone AND/OR circuit size is Omega(N^(delta^2 K/2)). Use delta=1/12, K=floor(N^(1/4)). Hence log2 monotone size is Omega(N^(1/4) log N), equivalently Omega(n^(1/8)(log n)^(7/8)) on the encoded lengths above. This is an exponential lower bound only for circuits whose restriction to this positive edge projection is monotone (or convertible to monotone at constant factor). Monotonicity of the FUNCTION does NOT imply monotone implementation. Unrestricted SAT circuits can use negation and arbitrary Boolean gates; no removal of that premise is claimed.

## 3. Negation width requirement via elementary monotone masking

Related established result: Jukna and Lingas, Lower bounds for Boolean circuits of bounded negation width, JCSS 129 (2022), 90–105, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcss.2022.05.003 and https://web.vu.lt/mif/s.jukna/papers/negwidth_abs.html . The following elementary masking argument is included for self-contained transfer (not a priority claim, and weaker than their optimized conversion).

Represent a signed AIG computing the restricted clique function by a De Morgan circuit with AND/OR gates and signed primary literals only, by retaining both phases of each gate when needed (at most 2s binary gates from s AIG gates; constants and input literals free). General B2 circuits can first be simulated by constant-factor signed AIGs, but the proof-trace property below refers to the specified converted circuit. A formal accepting term is obtained by choosing one child at each OR and both at each AND, unfolding the DAG as needed, collecting input literals; discard inconsistent terms. Shared subcircuits may have different occurrences in a trace. For a monotone function f, a minterm is an inclusion-minimal accepting set M of positive inputs. Every formal term accepting on M contains exactly M as its distinct positive variables: positive set P subset M and negative set B disjoint M; setting just P to one still satisfies the term, so minimality forces P=M. Define w=max over minterms M of the minimum number of distinct negative variables in any accepting term on M. This is the relevant minterm negation width, not a count of NOT gates.

Let b=max minterm size and T=#minterms. For any mask Z of input variables, build a monotone circuit C_Z with the same AND/OR DAG by replacing positive literals in Z by zero, negative literals in Z by one, and negative literals outside Z by zero. Other positive literals unchanged. Every accepting formal term of C_Z comes from a term whose negative set B lies in Z and whose positive set P avoids Z. Consequently if C_Z(x)=1, the original circuit accepts on x with Z cleared to zero, and f(x)=1 by monotonicity. Thus C_Z<=f pointwise even when original terms contain contradictions (they cannot survive the mask).

For each M choose a witnessing term with |B|<=w. A random Z including each input independently with probability p=1/(b+1) contains B and avoids M with probability p^|B|(1-p)^|M| >= (b+1)^(-w)/e. That event guarantees C_Z accepts M. Choose L=ceil(e*(b+1)^w*(ln T+1)) independent masks. Union bound gives positive probability all T minterms covered. OR their monotone circuits to compute f exactly (lower bound by minterm coverage and monotonicity, upper by soundness). Thus mono-size <= L*(2s+1) for an original s-gate signed AIG. For clique, b=binom(K,2), T=binom(N,K). Combining with Section 2,
    w log2(b+1) >= Omega(K log N) - O(log(s+1)+log(log(T+1)+1)).
For s<=N^A with any fixed A and K=floor(N^1/4), this yields w=Omega(K). Therefore some minimal accepting graph (exactly the edges of a K-clique) has the property that EVERY accepting formal term in the specified De Morgan conversion uses Omega(K) distinct negative edge literals. It is not enough for the circuit to contain negations that never contribute to those traces. This is a structural requirement on small circuits, not a lower bound on distinct negation gates or total unrestricted gates. The same negative inputs may be reused by many traces; no additive charge across minterms is justified.

## 4. A different structural constraint: widespread inconsistent path phases

Use a different clique size K=ceil(log2 N), with the same edge coordinates if common m chosen as in Section 1. Let L=K^4 (for sufficiently large N, L<=N). In the signed AIG after fixing all nonedge physical inputs, call a free edge primary binate in path phase if it has both even- and odd-complement paths to the output (including output phase). This is syntactic, not semantic nonmonotonicity of the output. For any essential input with paths of only one sign, that sign must be positive: dual-rail De Morgan conversion makes the output monotone in that input if only positive literals occur, antitone if only negative literals occur; clique is monotone and essential in every edge for 2<=K<=N.

Form a graph B on the N vertices with an edge for each binate primary. If there exists an independent vertex set U of size L in B, fix all edge primaries outside the induced complete graph on U to zero. The resulting function is Clique_(L,K) (no clique of size>=2 can use an isolated outside vertex). The remaining free primaries all have only positive paths in the original hard slice. In its dual-rail De Morgan conversion, no negative literal of these inputs occurs in the live output cone; other literals are fixed constants. Therefore it is a monotone circuit of size <=2s. With L=K^4, K=L^(1/4), the cited lower bound gives log size Omega(K log K)=Omega(log N log log N), inconsistent with s<=N^A for every fixed A and large N. Thus alpha(B)<L.

Elementary independent set bound: random vertex ordering, choose each vertex preceding all its neighbors. This is independent and expected size sum_v 1/(deg(v)+1) >= N^2/(2|E(B)|+N) by Cauchy-Schwarz. Hence
    |E(B)| > (N^2/L - N)/2 = Omega(N^2/(log N)^4).
So a polynomial-size signed AIG for the hard slice must have at least that many distinct physical edge inputs with both path phases. The condition holds after the chosen fixed-metadata restriction, not merely in the full switchable circuit (where positive/negative selector contexts already force binate paths). It is separate from Section 3, uses a different K, and no addition of their obligations is licensed. Many binate inputs may share the same few internal inversion/reconvergence sites. In particular this does not imply a superlinear gate count or many distinct NOT gates. It does rule out candidate topologies whose negative-path support is concentrated on too few edge coordinates after the hard restriction.

The prior two-gate deletion/core reserve remains available in unit mode on these same physical edge coordinates, but its linear charge cannot be added to a monotone bound unless the surviving core is actually proved monotone or has bounded negation width after the actual deletion. Neither small circuit size nor a large binate count proves that. A useful future target is a restriction-stable proof-trace width bound in terms of a genuinely scarce original gate resource; naive summing of negative literals, paths, or repeated minterm witnesses is invalid. The external monotone theorem is not a Lean theorem here; independent audits remain outstanding. L-clb-036 remains open.
