## 1. Independent paper audit of native scalar proof

Verdict: sound, subject to explicit scope/degenerate-case clarifications below. The proof is distinct from, and cleaner in its first-gate substitution step than, the earlier [scalar proof](first_gate_translation_proof_handoff.md). No native replay or Lean invocation was independently performed for this audit. The Lean source was read, not compiled here.

Affine lift: for any t-AND XOR-AND circuit, introduce private independent coordinates for the two primary affine contributions at each AND, and additional independent coordinates for the final direct affine contribution. The lifted ANDs depend only on their own private pair and previous outputs; the actual circuit is an affine pullback. Translates of the pullback are pullbacks of translates, giving the inequality on ranks. For native templates with additional shared input variables Y, an invertible affine substitution of the first pair absorbs its Y terms. The first pair must occur only in its first gate after normalization; this follows from the private-coordinate construction and is essential. Do not apply the identity to an arbitrary circuit in which the first gate's primary variables are also read directly later without first lifting.

Exact substitution: write H'=A(z)+y E(z), H=A(z)+ab E(z), with a,b fresh independent and absent from A,E. Let M'=span of all translates of H', and U=span of translates of E. Then U=D_y M' and U is a subspace of M'. Let phi replace y with ab. Old translations of H give phi(T_v H'); differences in a or b produce b T_v E and a T_v E, and the double difference produces T_v E. These last functions equal phi(T_v E) and lie in phi(M'). Conversely every translate of H belongs to phi(M')+aU+bU by expanding (a+epsilon)(b+eta); old translates and the constant-in-y E terms also generate phi of every y-translate of H'. The sum is direct by the four fresh-monomial coefficient blocks: phi(M') has only the empty and ab blocks, whereas aU,bU are the a,b blocks. Phi is injective because these coefficient blocks recover A and E. Hence the claimed exact identity
    rho(H)=rho(H')+2 dim D_y M'
holds without assumptions on E (including E=0 or E=1). In particular direct output use of the first gate is handled automatically. This proves step 3 of the native handoff, not just numerical consistency.

Template preservation: replacing the first gate by independent input y deletes exactly one AND and makes any old read of that gate a raw affine primary contribution. No new AND is needed. A fresh lift of the smaller circuit if necessary yields the same class. Thus the scalar induction is over all circuits/affine pullbacks, not only over the same number of primary inputs. D_y^2=0, M' is invariant, rank-nullity gives 2 rank D_y <=dim M'. Zero-AND affine base has rho<=2. All t>=0 follow. The matching monomial on t+1 fresh bits has rank 2^(t+1) (its translates span all square-free divisors) and uses t ANDs. For any Boolean function of degree d, take a maximal degree support S; D_T f for T subset S have independent leading restrictions after setting variables outside S to zero (restriction of f has top monomial S, the 2^d derivative polynomials are triangular in degree/subset order). Hence rho>=2^d. This verifies the stated degree comparison.

Clarifications: rho-2 is the mixed rank of a NONCONSTANT function's translation lift, not of the zero or one function. For t=0 the maximum mixed rank is zero; the constant cases also have zero mixed rank. The logarithmic corollary is stated for rho>0 (zero function has MC=0). The native Lean file TranslationHalving.lean verifies square-zero halving and a conditional doubling recursion according to the native execution report; the first-gate identity and connection of the recursion to circuits are not yet formalized. Its hpre hypothesis is the bound for the smaller instance, not a standalone theorem giving the bound without the induction premise.

The whole-family substitution identity for dim M and dim m M follows by the same direct sum argument using the sum over all generators. For the augmentation subspace, the phi block is phi(m M'), because old differences and fresh double differences produce it, and D_y M' is contained in m M'. The a,b blocks are again U. This agrees with the earlier exact first-gate joint update. No independent audit of the native general composition identity, proposed subgroup bound, or hill-climb claims is asserted in this note.

## 2. Joint problem: the supposedly open mixed-direction step already has a paper proof

The new native handoff labels Part 2 open. However, [joint_augmentation_rank_handoff.md](joint_augmentation_rank_handoff.md) was already present at merge 0345f19. It proves the stronger statement: for the generic s-gate family W_s over ANY characteristic-two field k, every augmentation-ideal element A has
    rank(A|W_s)<=2^s.
Taking A=D_v gives the missing Part 2. It is not a proof by separate rank-nullity on the full family. It uses the additional freedom to form arbitrary augmentation operators over rational-function extensions. This is exactly what avoids the previously listed failed approaches.

Self-recheck of the prior joint proof (NOT an independent audit of our own proof):
- First-gate delete ab, let W be the old suffix family, L=D_s the shift induced by dependencies on the first gate, B=span(1,LW), Phi(w)=w+ab Lw. The exact full family space is Phi(W) directsum k*ab directsum aB directsum bB for nonempty suffix. Every fresh primary coordinate belongs to W, by differencing the appropriate gate in its other private coordinate then subtracting its earlier gate dependencies.
- Write arbitrary new augmentation operator T=A+D_a P+D_b Q+D_aD_b R. Work over k(eta) if necessary to make P+eta a unit, then multiply by its inverse. This operation does not assume P is a unit originally; rank at specialization eta=0 cannot exceed generic rank. In normalized form P=1, the image coordinates of Phi(w)+a u+b v+c ab are x=(A+RL)w+u+Qv+c r0, y=A u+QLw+c q0, z=A v+Lw+c, with y=(A+RL)x+Qz. Projection onto (x,z) is therefore injective on the image. Its x-image is AW+B and kernel image is span(1,L A^-1(B)); the AB contribution lies in that span since A^2=0 and A,L commute. These formulas also handle arbitrary Q,R.
- For L nonzero, B=LW, let l=rank L, K=ker L, and q=rank of the induced A on K/B. The dimension computation in the prior proof gives rank(T)=2l+q. Check nonvanishing of L A^-1(B): choose a primary coordinate z_j with s_j=1; then Lz_j=1 and Az_j is constant, so z_j in A^-1(B). This is why the exact formula is valid for this circuit module, not an arbitrary abstract module. For L=0 the exact projection formula gives rank(T)<=rank(A)+2, which suffices.
- Matrix pencil L+xi A on W over k(xi): pivot on the identity block mapping a complement of ker L to im L; append any nonsingular q-minor of the induced A on K/B. The determinant's leading coefficient at order xi^q is that nonzero minor, since off-diagonal blocks contribute order >=2 after pivot elimination. Thus rank(L+xi A)>=l+q. This pencil lies in the old augmentation ideal; the strengthened induction hypothesis at s-1 gates applies over the extension field. Hence l+q<=2^(s-1), giving 2l+q<=2^s. The L=0 case also follows for s>=2. Base s=1 has dimension four and square-zero gives rank<=2. Specialization covers arbitrary P. No gap found in this recheck.
- The first-gate joint update adds 2 dim B to J_old, dim B<=2^(s-1); J_1=2. Therefore J_s<=2^(s+1)-2. The scalar chain gives equality in the generic class. The exact generator count dim W_s/(m W_s)=s follows from the two exact direct-sum dimension identities, not just from samples.

Native templates with extra shared inputs do not create a gap in the above proof: the full family of nonlinear gate outputs of any such template is a common affine pullback of a private-ingress generic gate family. Directional rank cannot increase under the pullback, since D_v of a pullback is pullback of D_(linear part(v)) on the generic family; likewise an arbitrary augmentation operator maps to an augmentation operator via the induced group algebra homomorphism. Joint mixed factor rank also cannot increase. Affine generators such as the extra y in the native deleted family add at most the constant 1 to the directional image. If the image was nonzero it already contains 1; if zero the new rank is at most 1, within the claimed cap. For no nonlinear gates handle affine-only case directly. The exact quotient generator dimension s is claimed for the private generic family, NOT for arbitrary pullbacks or arbitrary added affine generators.

Status should be reconciled as: scalar independently paper-audited here; general joint paper proof exists and has been self-rechecked, but the joint proof still awaits independent native-agent audit and Lean formalization. No claim of formal verification or literature priority. The subgroup conjecture is separate and not needed. Do not repeat directional hill climbs as if the matrix-pencil approach had not been developed.

## 3. Strategic disposition

Agree with the native agent's recommendation to close rank-only lower bounds as a primary route to L-clb-036: for functions on n bits the translation space sits in a 2^n-dimensional function space. The logarithmic bound is therefore at most n-1, and analogous common mixed-space bounds are also capped by input-side dimension. Even the sharp joint theorem does not itself produce superlinear size. A useful distinction: this is a ceiling on THESE aggregate rank/translation bounds, not an impossibility theorem for every conceivable rank-based structural argument. The later parallel-fiber, graph-reserve and polar-coset methods count distinct gate IDs under additional structural premises, but their present SAT witnesses still have linear upper constructions. Do not infer any superlinear lower bound from them.
