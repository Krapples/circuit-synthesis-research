Main exploratory outcomes, with their limits:
- Translation rank ceiling and nonlinear periods: precise local capacities, but the exponential rank-vs-gate ceiling can yield at most linear numerical lower bounds in the input count.
- Gate elimination, first-branch separation, and multiport residual/operand ledger: valid original-ID accounting; one first branch per primary yields only linear excess charge; XOR basis distinction matters.
- Packed separators/well-linkedness and Andreev: strong restricted graph/formula bounds, not gate complexity for arbitrary high-sharing DAGs. Negative controls are efficient on their promise.
- Switchable incidence: one fixed physical payload set can express programmable selector/dictionary/tag contexts and fixed-metadata arbitrary existential 3-CNF incidence. This is a reusable research platform, not a proof of hardness from NP-completeness.
- Private compression and mixed-support asymmetry: conditional polynomial lower bounds, additive disjoint private charges, robust per-slot ingress routes; early mixing, high-capacity gates and shared endpoints evade the unrestricted conclusion.
- Movable triple lookup: quadratic weighted demand even in hard incidence; explicit O(m ell) evaluator for the entire lookup promise is a decisive negative control.
- Fixed triangle, frontier rigidity and slack: all-cut matching residuals, simultaneous bidirectional ingress, exact abstract compression capacity, monotone tight-frontier BMM cost, nonmonotone permutation-slack restrictions. None requires every small circuit to expose the hard frontier. Raw wide bypass, early Z mixing, arbitrary product encodings remain. The latest slack inequalities only imply a linear number of prefix gates. Near-width port identity is false by explicit monotone encoder counterexample.

Common missing step: prove an unrestricted computational cost for shared original gate resources, or prove a size-preserving reduction to an architecture already excluded. More ports, cuts, residuals or distinct roles alone do not provide disjoint gate counts.

## Particularly relevant literature found in this review

Mishra and Santhanam, *An Explicit 4n-O(log(n)) Size Lower Bound against General Boolean Circuits*, ECCC TR26-260 revision 1, Nov 3, 2026: https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2026/260/ . Inspected abstract and introductory theorem/method (PDF pp. 1-3), not a full independent proof audit or publication-status review. It reports a 4n-O(log n) B2 lower bound for an explicit P function, via affine dispersers, with a dynamically changing independent-parameter representation rather than only physical-input substitutions. This is not a SAT bound and should not be numerically compared to counted-NOT SAT without transfer. This method is a closer match for our restriction-stable gate-ID work than further scalar cut bounds. Read the full proof, its potential, invariants, allowed substitutions and exceptional cases before claiming any adaptation.
Golovnev, Hirsch, Knop, Kulikov, *On the Limits of Gate Elimination*, ECCC TR16-119 rev1 https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2016/119/ : the abstract states formal barriers for certain fixed-step inductive substitution arguments. Read exact hypotheses before applying to our context-dependent parameter scheme; not an impossibility theorem for all gate elimination.
Williams, *Non-Uniform ACC Circuit Lower Bounds*, https://people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/acc-lbs-ccc.pdf : algorithms-to-lower-bounds route. Our practical finite exact-synthesis improvements are not the required worst-case circuit satisfiability time improvement. Treat as a separate longer-term literature/algorithmic exploration, not an established bridge to SAT.

## Proposed next phase (recommendation, not authorization to modify production)

1. Consolidate and verify. Build a short index of the strongest unrestricted results, conditional results with explicit premises, and counterexamples; formalize the stable-ID operand ledger and pair-cofactor first-branch lemma first. Obtain an independent audit of the switchable physical grammar and its pair witnesses. Reproduce exact locally checked SAT statements, check current upstream matching desk gap/imports before any submission. If no matching gap, propose the precise theorem rather than submit an unrelated statement. Keep counted-NOT, signed AIG and B2 coefficients distinct.

2. Study independent-parameter gate elimination. Reproduce the published/preprint 4n result's local transition rules and potential on small examples. Define a legal SAT restriction family parameterized by independent bits, with substitutions allowed to change their representation. Determine exactly which moves preserve a residual hard-family invariant and how many distinct actual original gate IDs are removed net of new encoding gates. Ordinary SAT is not an affine disperser on all its physical bits: many legal fixed-metadata subcubes are constant. Need a SAT-specific robust invariant, perhaps a set of selectable contexts with legal completion and surviving genuine existential cores. No claim that a simple affine embedding suffices. For any explicit hard function F_d=SAT_n composed with map phi, C(SAT_n)>=C(F_d)-cost(phi) in the same basis; budget every repeated/computed coordinate. Even a 4d-O(log d) B2 lower bound on F_d will not improve a 2n SAT bound if n is much larger than d or map cost is high.

3. Aim for a concrete intermediate unrestricted improvement. Establish a new provable gate-elimination improvement in the actual SAT model (e.g. a positive leading-coefficient gain over 2n on common lengths, or extend the checked bound to other relevant even length classes). Treat this as an achievable research milestone, not a sufficient route to superlinear bounds. Use exact synthesis/certificates on small parameterized residuals to falsify candidate local transitions and assess their marginal benefit over existing native pruning. Keep nonuniform synthesis time distinct from circuit-SAT algorithm time. Do not claim every small SAT circuit has a minimum-width triangle frontier or low support asymmetry.

4. Main long-term question: can a restriction-compatible potential grow with scale? For a recursively preserved hard family on d independent parameters, a true recurrence C(d)>=C(d-b)+Omega(b*d^epsilon) over a constant fraction of the parameters would yield Omega(d^(1+epsilon)), if basis, legal embedding length, net gate IDs, and preservation are all certified. This is a target condition only. Constant-factor local savings, or a sum of full prefix bounds, cannot establish it. Before pursuing a candidate invariant, test against the existing linear/quasilinear lookup, two-read normal form, permutation network, orbit compression, and triangle wide-bypass controls. Stop or reframe if the proposed measure is satisfied by those controls at low cost. A proof of a size-preserving normal form that forces one of our already excluded architectures is another possible path, but that normalization is itself a major missing theorem.

5. Keep two complementary secondary tracks: guarded monotone SAT on the single Hamming layer (the verified exponent-preserving bridge, not a direct CLIQUE transfer) and exact synthesis/native local structural tools. The latter should be judged by reproducible solver performance and certified exact results, not by whether it alone settles an asymptotic conjecture. Algorithmic lower bounds are an optional distinct research track only if a worst-case complexity theorem can be identified and proved. Prioritize collaboration and independent verification over more unaudited handoffs.

Suggested allocation for the next research phase: 50% independent-parameter/restriction research, 25% formal audit and consolidation, 15% exact-synthesis falsification controls, 10% alternate-route reading.
