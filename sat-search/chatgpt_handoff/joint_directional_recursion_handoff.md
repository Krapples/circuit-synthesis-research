# Joint translation module bounds and exact directional recursion

Date: 2026-11-26. Extends [first-gate scalar proof](first_gate_translation_proof_handoff.md) and [joint rank update](joint_translation_rank_handoff.md). Paper bounds and exact directional identity, plus finite regressions. No independent audit, Lean formalization, priority claim, or unrestricted superlinear SAT lower bound. L-clb-036 remains open.

## 1. Scalar-to-joint shortcut fails

Generic three-gate template
    G1=ab, G2=(c+G1)d, G3=(e+G1)(f+G1).
The exact mixed rank of its seven nonzero scalar XOR masks in binary order is 2,6,6,6,6,8,8. Joint rank is 10. Hence the joint rank need not equal the maximum scalar-output rank in the same circuit. This is not a counterexample to either exponential ceiling. Indeed even allowing scalar coefficients over an extension field of GF(2) cannot fix this example: delete first gate; f=lambda2*cd+lambda3*ef, full translation rank <=6, difference plus lambda1 has affine degree <=1 and full translation rank <=2. The first-gate square-zero lemma works over any field of characteristic 2, rescaling a maximal-support derivative by its nonzero constant coefficient. For nonconstant f this gives full rank <=10 and mixed rank <=8; for f=0 the rank is at most 4. Direct GF(2) regression tests the eight masks. No extension-field computation is claimed.

## 2. Universal analytic joint ceiling

Let W be the span of all translates of a family of p generic gate outputs. Let D(W)=span of all D_u W where D_u=T_u+I is the translation difference. For p>=1, the family contains a nonconstant gate (each has its own fresh pair monomial), so 1 belongs to D(W) by iterated difference of a maximal-support ANF monomial. The common anchored mixed factor space is D(W) modulo constants, hence dim D(W)=J+1, where J is the joint mixed coefficient rank. The quotient W/D(W) is generated by the p original gate outputs, because each translate of a generator is congruent to that generator modulo D(W). Write k=dim(W/D(W))<=p. Thus dim W=J+1+k<=J+p+1. This is an upper bound, not an assumption that the original outputs are always independent in the quotient. All sampled cases below happen to have k=p; no universal equality proof claimed.

Every D_s is square-zero on W, so rank(D_s|W)<=floor((J+1+k)/2). Every nonzero translation-invariant function subspace contains 1: for a nonconstant member apply its maximum-degree iterated difference, and for a nonzero constant the claim is immediate over GF(2). Thus dim B=max(1,rank(D_s|W)) for B=span(1,D_s W). For p>=1 dim W>=4, so dim B<=floor((J+1+k)/2) without a max correction.

Apply previous exact FIRST-gate joint update J_new=J+2 dim B to a suffix of p gates:
    J_new <= J+2 floor((J+1+k)/2) <= J+2 floor((J+p+1)/2).
This is an unconditional bound for the generic family, irrespective of rank-two dependencies; the same bound on common actual mixed factor spaces follows by common ingress pullback. J is always even: base J1=2, update adds an even number. Therefore the recurrence for universal integer upper bounds b_t (even) is
    b_1=2, b_t=2 b_(t-1)+2 floor(t/2).
Closed form b_t=(7/3)*2^t-t-gamma_t, with gamma_t=4/3 for even t and 5/3 for odd t. Check base and the recurrence directly. This gives J_t<(7/3)*2^t for t>=1 by a paper proof alone.

If using the complete canonical five-gate joint enumeration from the previous handoff as the base, b_5=62. The recurrence gives b_t=(103/48)*2^t-t-gamma_t for t>=5, hence J_t<(103/48)*2^t. Values at 5..12: 62,130,266,540,1088,2186,4382,8776. The lower construction attains 2^(t+1)-2; asymptotic upper coefficient is only 103/96 times the conjectured coefficient (~7.29% higher). This is NOT a sharp joint theorem, and relies on finite enumeration for the improved coefficient. Scalar theorem already proved the exact ceiling 2^(t+1)-2 for single outputs without enumeration; do not replace that stronger scalar result with this weaker joint cap.

## 3. Exact directional recursion: both fresh bits and old shifts

Let W be the common full translation space of a nonempty generic suffix family F_i on old variables z. Prepend G0=ab, and let its coefficient shifts on the suffix be s. Write L=D_s, Phi(w)=w+ab Lw, B=span(1,LW). The new family consists of ab and Phi(F_i). As in earlier first-gate proof its full joint translation space is exactly the direct sum
    Wnew = Phi(W) directsum span(ab) directsum aB directsum bB.
Indeed old translations of Phi(F_i) generate Phi(W), fresh differences generate aLW,bLW and LW, translations of ab generate a,b,1, and 1=Phi(1). Fresh double shifts add only LW, already in Phi(W) because L^2=0. Uniqueness of the expansion in 1,a,b,ab proves directness (if constant coefficient w is zero, its ab graph coefficient also zero). B is translation invariant, B subset W, L B=0.

Take an arbitrary directional difference on the new cube, with fresh shifts epsilon,eta and old shift v. Put M=D_v, E=MW. M,L commute, square to zero, preserve W and B. Write d_new(epsilon,eta,v) for rank of this directional operator on Wnew.

Case A: epsilon=eta=0. On Phi(W), the difference is Phi(MW). It kills ab, and acts on aB,bB by applying M. These direct-sum images give
    d_new(0,0,v)=dim MW+2 dim MLW.
Because L is square-zero on the invariant space MW, dim MLW<=dim MW/2. Hence d_new<=2 dim MW. This preserves the directional conjecture inductively in directions with no shift in the first pair.

Case B: (epsilon,eta) nonzero. It suffices to consider (1,0): swapping a,b preserves ab; invertible affine transformation (a,b)->(a,a+b+1) also preserves ab and sends (1,1) to (1,0). These transformations leave the old variables and family fixed, so preserve directional rank under the corresponding conjugation.

In fresh case (1,0), write T_v=I+M. Apply the difference to
    Phi(w)+a p+b q+c ab, w in W,p,q in B,c in GF(2).
Its image in the direct sum Phi(W)+aB+bB has coordinates
    ( M w+T_v p, M p, L T_v w+M q+c ).
The first two coordinates determine a graph (x,Mx), where x runs over E+B: T_v is an automorphism of B, M^2=0, so Mx=Mp. Thus projection onto the first two coordinates has rank dim(E+B). Its kernel image consists of b-coordinate vectors with p=Mw in B. This equivalence follows from T_v^-1=T_v and T_v M=M; then Mp=0 and L T_v w=Lw since L(Mw)=0 for Mw in B. Let
    Z={w in W: Mw in B} = M^-1(B).
The remaining b-coordinate image is span(1,LZ,MB)=span(1,LZ), because MB=MLW subset LZ (MW subset ker M subset Z). Rank-nullity gives the exact formula
    d_new(epsilon,eta,v)=dim(E+B)+dim span(1,LZ), (epsilon,eta) != (0,0).
The second term is bounded by dim(B intersect ker M)=dim B-rank(M|B): LZ subset B and MLZ=LMZ=0 since MZ subset B and LB=0. The constant 1 also lies in B intersect ker M.

If M=0 or M=L (v=0 or v=s suffices), then E subset B, Z=W and the exact formula is 2 dim B. Hence assuming the old directional conjecture for s, the new directional conjecture holds for these cases. Together with Case A, the only remaining direction type is nonzero fresh shift and distinct nonzero old shifts s,v inducing potentially different operators. If s=0 and v arbitrary, B=span1, LZ=0, so rank is dim(E+span1)+1; this case can be analyzed separately and poses no generic coupled-product issue. For p=0 (empty suffix), W=0, new family ab only, directional rank 0 for zero direction or 2 for nonzero fresh shift. Handle separately.

Remaining sufficient condition for joint conjecture: for every p-gate suffix and two distinct nonzero directions s,v,
    dim(MW+B)+dim(span(1,L M^-1(B))) <= 2^(p+1),
assuming the old directional conjecture. This is now an exact two-direction module problem; independently bounding the terms by separate rank-nullity may be too loose. No proof of this condition claimed. It accounts for shared computations once using common W rather than sum of separate scalar ranks.

## 5. Scope and next direction

The scalar-to-joint shortcut is refuted, but the first-gate joint approach has a quantitative unconditional ceiling and exact two-direction obstruction. Next study the remaining coupled inequality, perhaps via simultaneous structure of commuting square-zero translations on W and a strengthened induction on two-direction images. Do not extrapolate sampled evidence to general bounds or assume arbitrary modules behave like circuit-generated modules. For L-clb-036, the generic joint rank is still an exponential function of t, so its logarithm alone cannot establish a superlinear bound in SAT input size. No new SAT grammar or total gate lower bound this round. No production modifications or upstream submission. Independent audit and Lean verification remain outstanding.
