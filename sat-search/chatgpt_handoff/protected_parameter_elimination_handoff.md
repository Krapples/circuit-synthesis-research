# Protected-parameter elimination for switchable SAT: affine-fiber degree dispersion and legal rank-two exchange

## 1. Why the direct affine-disperser transfer fails

The source's theorem gives a B2 lower bound 4d-10delta for a d-input affine disperser of dimension delta. Its state includes a globally injective affine map, sparse uniquely solvable affine equations and an acyclic circuit, plus disjoint pairs of parameters subject to product zero. Write a=m-p (parameter count minus pair count) and W=s+4p+6I, where s includes the affine equation count and acyclic gate count, I counts used unpaired parameters. Lemma 4.2 gives either a monochromatic affine flat or an iteration with Delta a in {0,-1}, Delta W<=10 Delta a and strict progress. Their Remark 4.3 states that reweighting just these two potential terms cannot improve their leading coefficient. The P-computability hypothesis serves the algorithmic counterexample search; we use only the necessary disperser condition here.

Our fixed-hard-metadata incidence H_(m,ell), D=3m ell, has a monochromatic NO affine coordinate cube of dimension D-6ell for m>=2: fix one clause to three copies of signed index 0 and another to three copies of 1. All other clauses arbitrary. It also has a YES cube of dimension D-m: choose all witness variables true, pin the polarity bit of one literal per clause to positive, leave every other payload bit free. Thus it has no small-dimensional affine disperser property. More generally let Phi:F2^d -> F2^D be any injective affine map for fixed hard metadata, with at least one satisfying image point. Choose one satisfying witness at that point, then one true literal per group. Fix the entire ell-bit index of that chosen literal at its current value (at most m ell affine equations on parameters). Their common solution space through the original point has dimension >=d-m ell, or zero if that lower bound is negative. All formulas there have the SAME satisfying witness. If H composed with Phi is nonconstant, its minimum disperser dimension delta must satisfy delta>=max(1,d-m ell+1). If the composition is constant no disperser bound applies. Consequently the numerical expression 4d-10delta obtainable by the direct theorem is <=4m ell (even before paying for any affine preprocessing). This is a barrier to that particular direct transfer on this fixed-incidence layout, not an upper bound on SAT circuit size or a general impossibility for affine elimination. The physical switchable length n=Theta(m log m) has large overhead and this expression cannot improve the checked full SAT 2n-o(n) bound on these layouts. Free-complement B2 and counted-NOT coefficients must remain distinct.

## 2. A single affine protected-query family containing BOTH selector and hard modes

Reuse switchable_incidence_handoff.md without changing any physical coordinates or grammar. Let m=2^k>=2, 1<=ell<=k, P=2^ell, t=3m, D=t ell. Introduce independent data vector x consisting of t ell-bit slots. Introduce protected query vector y=(s_0,...,s_(t-1),lambda_0,...,lambda_(ell-1),p), length t+ell+1, where s_i=1 means activate slot i in unit mode. Use bitwise XOR (not integer addition) in
    tag_i = (1 XOR p) XOR ((1 XOR s_i)*(i+2)).
Here the first term is a bit in the lowest tag position, and the second term is either the fixed bit pattern i+2 or zero. Since t is even, flipping the lowest bit permutes 2,...,t+1; every possible tag is legal. Keep physical literal signs positive. Program dictionary unit sign h(a)=lambda dot a over F2. Keep all other physical metadata constants except the mode sign p and tag digits just specified.

This maps the (D+t+ell+1)-bit joint cube AFFINELY AND INJECTIVELY into actual valid paired SAT words. All low payload coordinates have single positive copy as before. Recover p from mode sign, lambda_j from dictionary sign at a=2^j, s_i from any nonzero higher bit of i+2 in its tag (complement), x from payload digits. Q is a power of two and all identifiers retain fixed width, so there is no arithmetic carry mixing low payload and upper tag. If actual ordinary B2 preprocessing is desired, all physical output coordinates except the dictionary linear forms and lowest tag bits are constants or signed literals. Generate all P dictionary parities in at most P-ell-1 XOR gates by recursively removing one set bit, and t/2 odd-tag positions each need at most one XOR of p and s_i. Total affine encoding cost <=t/2+P-ell-1 B2 gates with free literals. Count those gates/equations in any transfer; no free arbitrary affine preprocessing claim.

Two contained contexts:
- p=1: tag 0 when s_i=1, unique fresh tag i+2 otherwise. SAT verdict is F(x,s,lambda)=AND_{i:s_i=1}(lambda dot x_i). Inactive slots are unforced and mutually disjoint. Empty selected set gives true. Dictionary h is common across all active slots.
- p=0 and s_i=1 for all i: every tag equals 1, hence SAT verdict is the full fixed-metadata H_(m,ell)(x), with genuine existential shared witnesses. The dictionary, though varying, is irrelevant to this hard mode.
Other query settings are legal but no simplified semantic formula for their verdict is assumed. This embedding retains the exact hard-incidence promise on the same physical coordinates while adding only t+ell+1 protected parameters, not one independent bit for every physical metadata coordinate. For ell=k the hard subfamily is the previously proved NP-complete incidence language; no new NP-completeness or padding proof is needed.

## 3. Relative affine-fiber degree dispersion

Let U be ANY affine subspace of (x,y) whose projection onto the whole query cube is surjective. Write V={v:(v,0) belongs to the direction space of U}; let a=dim V. Each query fiber is x=b(y)+V, with the SAME direction V but possibly query-dependent affine offset b(y). This is strictly weaker than requiring data coordinates to be independent of query in the affine representation.

For each bit coordinate j in a slot, define the linear projection L_j:V -> F2^t collecting coordinate j from all t slots, and its rank r_j. The combined projections are the identity up to ordering, hence sum_j r_j>=a. Pick j with r=r_j>=ceil(a/ell); choose r slot rows whose linear forms on V are independent. Choose the legal query p=1, lambda=e_j, s the indicator of these r selected slots. Surjectivity guarantees that this query has a nonempty fiber; its offset may depend on the chosen query and is irrelevant. On that fiber the selected r affine functions are independent in their linear parts, so their simultaneous value map onto F2^r is surjective. The SAT residual is the AND of these r affine forms. It has EXACT algebraic degree r: after an invertible affine basis change it is the product of r distinct independent coordinates, and algebraic degree is invariant under invertible affine changes (each substitution cannot increase degree, apply inverse for equality). For r>=1 it has both truth values; its true set has exactly 2^(a-r) points. Thus
    max_{legal query y} deg(F restricted to U_y) >= ceil(a/ell).
For a=0 only the trivial degree floor is claimed. In particular if a>ell, a globally affine output on U is impossible, and if a>2ell, a globally affine-or-one-AND-of-affine-forms output on U is impossible. This holds in either basis and for affine maps with arbitrary metadata drift in x. It is a new relative stopping invariant, not a scalar affine disperser. The terminal output premise is a degree bound, not an assumption that the remaining circuit has small size. The full family has constant query slices (e.g. p=1,s=0) and remains far from an ordinary affine disperser.

Paired-domain extension: maintain d independent data parameters and the protected query coordinates unchanged, with h disjoint pairs among data parameters, each constrained to product zero. Use a jointly injective affine parameter map (u,y)->(x(u,y),y), so the data direction map is injective for each y. Choose one member of each data pair to set zero, identically for every query. The resulting domain is an affine subspace with full query projection and a=d-h data fiber dimension. Section 3 applies on this subspace, so the two simple terminal forms of the preprint are impossible whenever d-h>2ell. The choice of zero member can be arbitrary. This is enough to transfer the terminal contradiction, but does not transfer the full elimination algorithm.

## 4. A protected fair-system rank-two move

Model as in the cited paper: r equations, each at most three nonconstant terms, uniquely solve r affine auxiliary values in terms of all parameters, plus an acyclic B2 part. A data-parameter exchange can use an unpaired data variable x with nonzero coefficient in an auxiliary value Q=x+phi(other parameters), including protected queries. Exchanging their roles leaves the equation list untouched (up to an affine constant phase shift), and preserves invertibility: given the new parameters recover x uniquely and then every old auxiliary. It also preserves the query projection exactly and preserves all existing paired constraints because no paired or protected coordinate is exchanged. It does not require computing an arbitrary affine substitution as new gates. Initially the physical embedding's computed affine coordinates must have been paid for as in Section 2.

Suppose a minimal AND-type gate G has two affine parents (parameters or auxiliaries) whose linear parts have rank TWO when restricted to the UNPAIRED DATA coordinates. Then some 2x2 minor on those coordinates is invertible. Promote its two parents to independent unpaired data parameters by the corresponding basis exchanges, using only data pivots (already-unpaired data parents need no exchange; if one is such a parent, pivot the other outside it). If both are auxiliary, perform sequential Gaussian basis exchanges; rank two ensures an available second pivot. Existing pair variables and all y remain unchanged. Orient the two new parameters by the gate's fixed operand phases, moving affine constants in equations if necessary. The actual gate function is their product plus a fixed output phase.

Add a new data pair requiring their product zero. No original physical coordinate substitution is implemented as a new gate; we have changed the domain and parameter map. The gate is now constant throughout the new domain, and can be deleted with every successor gate becoming unary/constant (at least one successor if it is not output). If G is output, the relative terminal invariant already rules this out at a>2ell. The constant/alias propagation removes original units (gates or equations), no new IDs. The affine part remains uniquely solvable. The map restricted to the new pair domain is still jointly injective; setting one member of each pair to zero preserves full query projection.

Define W=s+4h+6I_D, with s counting affine equations and acyclic gates, h number of DATA pairs, I_D number of used unpaired data parameters; protected queries not counted in I_D. Before pairing both promoted parents are used. Basis exchanges preserve or do not increase I_D: each old pivot was used because its coefficient influences an actual parent, new promoted variable is used, and no unchanged unused parameter acquires a new direct occurrence when the equations are retained. Pairing removes both parent weights, increases h by one, and deletes at least two original gate units. Rerouting a deleted gate to constant/unary operand cannot make an unused unpaired parameter newly used. Thus
    Delta a=-1, Delta s<=-2, Delta h=1, Delta I_D<=-2,
    Delta W<=-2+4-12=-10.
This is a concrete protected version of the preprint's rank-two pairing move, not a new claim about their unrestricted case analysis. It works even when each parent affine value depends on metadata; only the two data-pivot gradients must be independent.

More generally an affine restriction with r independent pivots among unpaired data can be solved for those pivots while retaining all query and paired coordinates; rank r on those columns is a simple sufficient test. Affine semantic solvability alone does not imply a cost-free circuit implementation; the fair-system role exchange above is what justifies the cost accounting for actual parent values. Do not use an arbitrary external affine form as a free internal gate.

## 5. Exact remaining gap and negative control

The protected rank-two move and the relative terminal condition are NOT a full protected version of Lemma 4.2. A minimal AND may have rank one or zero on unpaired data, e.g. one parent a protected query bit. The published proof is permitted to exchange, restrict and pair any unpaired parameters, including our protected query parameters. Those moves could destroy query surjectivity and invalidate Section 3. We have not shown a replacement for those cases preserving the potential decrease.

Indeed the selector-only family in p=1 has an explicit small B2 circuit: compute lambda_j AND x_ij for each i,j, XOR-reduce each slot, OR with NOT s_i, then AND all t results. Gate count D+(D-t)+t+(t-1)=2D+t-1, with free input phases. Every initial minimal AND that computes a coordinate product has only one data-dependent parent; the rank-two protected move cannot handle these gates. Thus the degree-dispersion terminal condition by itself cannot justify a universal protected 4D-O(ell) lower bound; the selector-only family is a rigorous barrier, not a circuit for the entire affine embedded SAT family. In particular a proposed rule completing all the paper's steps using ONLY this terminal invariant and maintaining Delta W<=10 Delta a would contradict this explicit upper bound (take t growing and ell>=1). The p=0 context is indispensable for any stronger result.

For clarity: IF one could prove all transitions for the full embedded SAT family with W nonnegative, no increase in s, Delta W<=10 Delta a, strict progress and full query projection preserved until a<=2ell, then W0<=B+encoding_cost+6D would imply B>=4D-20ell-encoding_cost. This is only an accounting target, not a proved lower bound; even that coefficient on D would not exceed the current physical full-SAT bound on this high-overhead layout. To make progress toward the original objective we must either exploit the hard-mode semantics for the bad rank cases, design a denser transfer, or charge a different potential. No claim of a superlinear recurrence or improvement in the full unrestricted size. The source also explicitly gives the coefficient-4 limit for its stated two-weight potential; simply retuning weights is not the research task.

## 7. Next concrete task

Keep the affine protected family and actual query projection invariant. Classify minimal AND-type configurations by rank 0,1,2 on free unpaired data, including cases with paired parents, and determine whether hard-mode residuals can be used to find alternative rank-two or larger profitable moves. The selector-only counterexample must be tested against each proposed generic rule. One possible direction is a semantic invariant that tracks hard-mode parameter fibers after partial loss of selector controls, rather than demanding full query surjectivity forever; any loss must be charged explicitly and the remaining legal contexts proved available, not assumed. A second useful task is formalizing the affine exchange and relative degree stopping lemma in Lean (no need to formalize the whole gate elimination algorithm first). Focus on an actual unrestricted numerical improvement or a new net-gate removal rule, not further conditional triangle frontier bounds. L-clb-036 remains open.
