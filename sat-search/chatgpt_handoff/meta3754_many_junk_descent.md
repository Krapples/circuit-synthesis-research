## New paper argument

Fix a least lift-fixed exact AO2 circuit C and choose r maximal in junk G C (last dirty AND in gate order). Suspend only gate r to false, preserving every other equation, including all earlier dirty ANDs. Call this valuation W. A pivotal edge is an unordered edge whose pair input is rejected at the suspended output.

1. There is a pivotal edge for every gate of any least circuit. The false suspension is pointwise at most the original circuit by monotone forward induction, independently of how many junk gates exist. If every edge survived, the checked false-suspension compiler would delete at least the chosen gate and preserve the entire edge function by monotonicity and its edge minterms, contradicting leastness.
2. Suppose the pivotal set of r is exactly {a,b}, with G.Adj a b. Replace gate r in place by the raw conjunction x_a AND x_b. On its pair input the original r must be true (otherwise suspension would agree everywhere). Thus the replacement is pointwise at most the original r by monotonicity. All other values decrease, and soundness follows. All surviving edges remain true since the replacement is above false suspension; the unique lost edge restores the original fill and hence the entire evaluation there. Exactness follows from monotonicity. Same gate count gives leastness. Earlier gates and their values are unchanged; r and every later AND are clean.
3. The new circuit may not be lift-fixed. Normalize only the suffix: a dirty OR with a clean operand q can be deleted, redirecting its users to its other operand p. Append an OR joining the residual output to q. This remains exact (if q is false, the old and residual valuations coincide; if q is true, the original output and appended OR are true). Every retained wire decreases pointwise, so every retained clean gate remains clean. The prefix before r stays unchanged: a lift there would also apply to the original fixed point, since gate equations and earlier values are identical. Each lift increases the length of the clean OR tail by at least one; termination follows from the fixed gate count. No new dirty AND arises in the suffix. The resulting E is least, exact and lift-fixed, with junk G E = (junk G C).erase r.
4. If one could prove every last dirty AND admits this singleton pivotal-edge condition, or another exact-size clean replacement covering all pivotal edges, induction would reduce any positive junk count to one, contradicting oneJunkExact. **That condition is not proved.** Multiple pivotal edges are the genuine remaining obstruction; arbitrary monotone lower fills need not be computable by one earlier binary gate. Do not assume a one-junk raw-support decomposition in the presence of earlier junk gates.

## Hazard-set characterization of exact in-place descent (paper argument)

Run 46044043859 for 7c93f40 was still in progress when this research began. Do not supersede that run; this is a documentation-only follow-up, which does not trigger the path-filtered verification workflow.

There is a more general descent route than appending clean products. Let r be the maximum dirty AND index, and let W0,W1 be the original gate equations with r suspended to constant false/true, respectively. Write V for the original valuation, L for the unordered edges e with W0(e,output)=false, and H for all F-negative inputs x with W1(x,output)=true. Use the exact definition of L on edge pair inputs, not all positive inputs. W0 is below V, and V is below W1 by the checked evalExceptRel argument; furthermore evaluations with equal fill at a particular input agree at that input on every wire. Hence W0 is false at every negative input and W1 is true at every edge input. The original fill is true on L and false on H. L is nonempty by checked everyGateHasLostEdge.

**Exact one-hole criterion.** For an actual earlier-wire binary gate replacement with Boolean function f(x) (an AND or OR of two original wire values at p,q < n+r), its replayed circuit D is exact if and only if f(e)=true for every e in L and f(x)=false for every x in H. Necessity follows from equal-fill evaluation. For sufficiency: on a negative x, either f(x)=false (replay agrees with W0), or f(x)=true and x is outside H (replay agrees with W1). On an edge e, either f(e)=true (W1 accepts), or f(e)=false and e is outside L (W0 accepts). Monotonicity of the actual replacement circuit extends edge-input coverage to all F-positive inputs. No assumption of global pointwise decrease of the replacement is needed. The new circuit has the same number of gates, same output index, and the unchanged earlier prefix, so exactness gives leastness.

**Clean suffix for any exact replacement.** The existing Lean-checked cleanToOutput is already stated for arbitrary EvalExcept, with hskip requiring only that the hole r is not an originally clean gate. It does not require unique junk or monotonic decrease. Apply it to the valuation of the exact replacement D: every originally clean gate j remains below F, since truth propagates through the original unchanged clean OR equations to D's exact output. In particular all ANDs after r remain clean. If the replacement at r is an OR (it can be dirty), or if it is an AND below F, all suffix ANDs are clean. Then checked normalizeCleanAndSuffix (or the pending general descentOfCleanSuffix wrapper) produces a least lift-fixed E with exactly junk G E = (junk G C).erase r. Thus a fully exact OR replacement is enough for the descent; requiring a clean replacement *AND* is unnecessarily restrictive. This argument also works when the replacement grows some wire values on negative inputs outside H.

**Safe earlier wires and two-cover obstruction.** Define A = {w < n+r | V(x,w)=false for every x in H}, and for w in A let L_w = {e in L | V(e,w)=true}. A single w in A with L_w=L is impossible in a least circuit: bypass r to w, using erase_exactAO and the checked bypassEvalExcept (fill at the hole equals V(w) by bypass_val_lt); the same exact one-hole criterion proves the bypass circuit exact, strictly smaller. If two earlier wires p,q in A satisfy L_p union L_q=L, use OR(p,q) at r. It vanishes on H and covers L, so this is an exact one-step descent. A useful special case is when the singleton support S of the last dirty AND contains two vertices s,t hitting every pivotal edge: raw s,t are in A, since their truth implies original r by monotonicity and original r vanishes on H. OR(s,t) then works. If one such raw vertex hits all L, the one-wire bypass contradicts leastness. This handles multiple pivotal edges without appending new gates or assuming pure earlier OR providers. Every earlier clean wire is in A as well. For a minimum-positive-junk counterexample, one-junk impossibility and exact descent imply that no two safe earlier wires cover L. The full conjecture would follow from proving such a two-cover (or another exact clean suffix repair) always exists in the remaining many-junk setting. That structural covering theorem is still open.

### Reproducible local obstruction to relying only on clean products

On vertices c,s,t,d,e,f (raw indices 0..5), take edges c-s,c-t,d-e, with f isolated. This is bipartite and C4-free. Original binary gates (wire indices n+j, with n=6):

    6 = d AND f
    7 = s OR 6
    8 = 7 OR t
    9 = s OR e
    10 = 9 OR t
    11 = 8 AND 10
    12 = c AND 11
    13 = d AND e
    14 = 12 OR 13   (output)

The dirty AND indices are 0 and 5; all original gates are live and the circuit is lift-fixed. False suspension of r=5 gives pivotal edges c-s,c-t; its residual output is d AND e. Chargeable suffix indices are 5,6,8, so the clean-product budget allows at most one repair conjunction. No residual pair of wires (including raw and all suspended gate wires, allowing repetitions) is simultaneously clean and covers both pivotal edges. The only residual wires true at both edge inputs are c,8,10: c AND 8 accepts the negative {c,d,f}; c AND 10 accepts {c,e}; 8 AND 10 accepts the negative singleton {s}; repetitions are also dirty. However replacing gate 5 by s OR t is exact and decreases every wire pointwise. Executed exhaustive Boolean checks over all 64 assignments in the JS runtime: original exact=true; dirty=[0,5]; lost=[[c,s],[c,t]]; charges=[5,6,8]; residual all-true output=true; clean one-product covers=[] over all 120 unordered-with-repetition pairs of wires; OR rewrite exact=true; all wire values decrease=true; dirty after rewrite=[0]; original lift sites=[].

This is a deliberately **nonleast** local control: the four-gate clean circuit (s OR t), c AND (s OR t), d AND e, collector OR is strictly smaller. Therefore it refutes only attempts to derive the one-product cover from C4-freeness, exactness and lift-fixedness alone. It is not a counterexample to L-meta-3754 or evidence that a least circuit defeats the more general repair budget. It also explains why searching only for clean AND repairs can miss valid OR descents.

## Stronger nonleast controls: all local liveness and pivotal tests can hold

During active verification run 46044654103 at dc4cb54 (checked in progress), no Lean source or workflow was changed. This follow-up is outside the workflow path filters.

A matching on k disjoint edges has a monotone CNF for its edge function: conjoin all 2^k clauses that choose one endpoint from every edge. Build the clauses with shared OR prefixes and combine them by a left-deep binary AND chain. The last dirty AND conjoins all but the last clause; the final AND is clean. On an independent set that picks one endpoint per edge, precisely the complementary clause is false. Consequently each proper prefix of the clause conjunction is dirty. Every clause is essential. The final AND has the last dirty AND as its immediate operand, so false suspension makes the entire output false; all k edges are pivotal. The only charged indices at or after the hole are the last two ANDs.

For k=2,3,4, the new executable [matching control](meta3754_matching_descent_control.js) exhaustively evaluates all assignments, all single-gate false suspensions, all binary AND/OR replacements at the last dirty AND using earlier wires (including repeated operands), and all pairs of residual wires for one clean product. Results:

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 7 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 3 |
| 3 | 19 | 6 | 2 | 3 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 5 |
| 4 | 43 | 14 | 2 | 4 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 7 |

All gates live, every raw/gate wire function distinct, no lift sites, exactness confirmed. For each circuit exactly one unordered AND replacement is exact (the original operand pair). Residual output is false; with two charges the repair budget permits only one product, and there is none. The checker additionally constructs the strictly smaller edge-DNF comparator and confirms its exactness on the whole cube. All assertions passed in the JS execution runtime. Reproduction: node sat-search/chatgpt_handoff/meta3754_matching_descent_control.js .

The family gives a rigorous **obstruction to deriving the local cover conditions solely from exactness, C4-freeness, lift-fixedness, liveness, distinct wire functions, and individual pivotal-edge existence**. It is NOT an obstruction under full leastness. No theorem about minimum circuits is falsified.

Next substantive target: charge the exclusive upstream cone, not only false-valued suffix gates. A paper formulation: for a cut of dirty gates, suspend those gates simultaneously, identify the live residual output and repair-provider cones, and retain only their ancestor-closed selections after constant/alias simplification. A list of clean residual products covering all lost edges can be appended for at most 2p gates (2p-1 when output is identically false). If the total deleted count exceeds that cost, exactness plus monotonicity of the replay gives a strict contradiction to leastness. Unlike the prefix-preserving descent, this strict budget may remove several earlier dirty gates or unused true-valued OR providers. In the matching controls the entire original cone is disposable, and the DNF comparator is a strict saving. A general bound connecting exclusive-cone charges to a repair cover in least C4-free circuits remains open; do not equate removal of all junk at equal cost with proof every least circuit is clean. First finish active repair CI, then formalize hazard-set descent and investigate multi-hole/exclusive-cone charges. No new Lean claims or upstream submission.

## Global exclusive-cone repair (new Lean candidate)

Verified baseline: run 46044654103, job 140660763836, at dc4cb54579381408c53500c3a40575659f083f29 passed all research files, pinned upstream bridge, and 106 transitive axiom reports. Both lastJunkCleanRepairDescent and lastJunkOneProductDescent passed. Subsequent 1f6cfc5 changes only the handoff and matching-control JS, so the verified sources are unchanged at that baseline.

New candidate generalizes the strict repair budget to simultaneously false-suspended gates and selective residual compilation, without retaining the earlier prefix. EvalHoles and existsEvalHoles construct the multiple-hole valuation. holesCompare proves it pointwise below the original and that an all-true zero is identically zero. compileExclusiveHoles accepts a requested-wire predicate need and finite paid set: each requested live gate has only raw, requested, or zero inputs; a requested live gate outside paid has a zero operand. Then the compiler realizes all requested (and raw/zero) residual functions in at most paid.card binary gates. Unrequested live cones, including earlier ANDs, are omitted; zero values are symbolic, not actual constant gates. The proof is induction in the original index order and reuses the checked appendAvailableAnd/Or.

noExclusiveConeRepair appends a clean-product cover using these residual functions. A strict budget paid.card + 2p < original gate count contradicts leastness, or paid.card + 2p <= original count if the residual output is dead and p>0. Soundness follows from the multi-hole comparison plus product cleanliness; completeness from covering edge minterms and monotonicity. Unlike the suffix theorem, this does NOT assert that equality gives exactly one fewer dirty gate. exclusiveConeRawEdgeLowerBound specializes to a list of raw edge repairs and proves the necessary global lower bound in the opposite direction. It applies to the matching controls: suspend the last dirty AND, the residual output is identically false, choose need=false, paid=empty, and the entire matching edge list as repairs. The original 7/19/43 gates violate the resulting <=3/5/7 bound. This example is a paper instantiation of the new general candidate, not a checked Lean instantiation yet.

The next obstruction is quantitative and graph-specific: for an arbitrary least C4-free circuit with dirty ANDs, select holes/needed residual cones and prove a strictly cheaper clean-product cover (or a prefix-preserving exact-size descent). Nothing yet establishes this universally. The hazard-set OR criterion remains a paper argument; prioritized global cone accounting because the matching family defeats local replacement even with all gates pivotal.
